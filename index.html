How it works

Hold the shortcut.

Press and hold Ctrl Alt Space in any app. A small pill shows that it is listening. You can change the shortcut, or switch to press-to-start, press-to-stop.

Say what you mean.

Speak naturally, with the ums and restarts. Your speech is transcribed by Sarvam's Saaras model and tidied up: filler words out, punctuation and casing in.

Release, and it appears.

The finished text is inserted at your cursor. Your clipboard is restored afterwards, so what you copied earlier is still there.

Features

Made for people who write all day.

  1. 01Types at your cursor

    Works in editors, browsers, chat apps and mail. Insert by paste, or by typing characters if an app blocks paste.

  2. 02Indian languages and English

    Built on Sarvam, which is trained for India's languages. Pick one, or let it detect, and use code-mix for Hinglish-style speech.

  3. 03Automatic clean-up

    Removes fillers and false starts and fixes punctuation. Choose to keep your tone, or go professional or casual.

  4. 04Personal dictionary

    Teach it names, product terms and jargon so they are spelled your way every time.

  5. 05Snippets

    Say a short trigger phrase and get your full text: addresses, links, sign-offs, replies you repeat.

  6. 06Tone per app

    Casual in chat, professional in email. ScreamFlow notices the active app and applies the tone you set for it.

Privacy and control

Your key. Your machine. Your history.

ScreamFlow has no account and no server of ours. You bring a Sarvam API key and the app talks to Sarvam directly.

  • Key stored in Windows Credential Manager,not in a settings file and not in the interface.
  • Audio goes to Sarvam only.It is recorded while you hold the shortcut and sent for transcription and clean-up. No other services.
  • History, dictionary and snippets stay on your PC,in a local database you can search and clear.
  • Honest failures.If clean-up fails you get your raw transcript with a visible notice, never a silent substitute.

Install in four steps

  1. Run ScreamFlow_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe.
  2. Open ScreamFlow and go to Settings.
  3. Paste your Sarvam API key and choose Test connection.
  4. Click in any text field, hold the shortcut and speak.

About the Windows warning

This early build is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it does not recognise the app. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need an account or a subscription?

There is no ScreamFlow account. You need your own Sarvam AI API key, and any usage is billed by Sarvam under your own plan.

Where does my voice go?

Your audio is recorded only while the shortcut is active, then sent to Sarvam for transcription and text clean-up. ScreamFlow does not send it anywhere else.

Will it work in every app?

It inserts text the way a keyboard paste does, so most apps work. Some elevated or protected windows can refuse input from another program. In that case the text is placed on your clipboard and you are told so. A full app-by-app test matrix is still being completed.

How accurate and how fast is it?

That depends on your microphone, your language and your connection. We have not published benchmark numbers yet and will not quote any until they are measured.

Can I change the shortcut?

Yes. The default is Ctrl+Alt+Space. You can set your own, and choose between hold-to-talk and press-to-start, press-to-stop. Esc cancels a recording.

Is there a Mac or Linux version?

Not yet. ScreamFlow is Windows-only for now.
